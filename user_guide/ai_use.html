0D viewers DAQmxAI and DAQmxDualAI

These plugins acquire one (DAQmxAI) or two (DAQmxDualAI) analog inputs of a NI card, as a voltage between -10 V and 10 V (differential terminals). They have been ported from the pydaqmx branch with the same names and settings, and tested against a fake of the nidaqmx layer only, not yet on hardware.

Configuration

  • Display type: 0D gives the mean value of the samples, 1D gives the trace as a function of time.

  • Frequency Acq.: the sampling frequency, in Hz.

  • Nsamples: the number of samples of one acquisition.

  • AI (DAQmxAI) or AI0 and AI1 (DAQmxDualAI): the analog input channels, something like Dev[X]/ai[Y]. The two channels of DAQmxDualAI are acquired together, with the same clock.

Use

The hardware averaging of PyMoDAQ is available: the acquisition is repeated and averaged. For other settings of the inputs (range, termination, current or temperature), use the general NIDAQmx viewer.
